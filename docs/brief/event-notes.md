## Organizers
- Google Developer Groups (GDG) on Campus at Virginia Tech. VP: Evan. President: Marcus. Tech leads/judges ran the workshop.
- Open to all majors and skill levels.

## Theme
- **AI for Housing, focused on the DMV** (DC / Maryland / Virginia). Every project must be housing-related and use AI.
- Example problem areas from the slides: affordability, rent, housing search. Any housing problem is allowed, not only the listed ideas.

## Schedule
- Sat: opening ceremony → judges' workshop (track details) + team formation in the room next door → hacking starts 11:00 AM → pizza lunch.
- Sun: 10:00 AM breakfast (donuts) → **submissions open 11:00 AM, hard deadline 12:30 PM** (late = not judged; ~90-minute window, cannot submit Saturday night) → **2:00 PM closing ceremony and winners**.

## Tracks
- **GDG "Best Overall AI for Housing"**: everyone is automatically entered. 1st, 2nd, 3rd place.
- **MLH tracks**: Best Open-Source AI Project, Best Use of Gemma 4, Best Use of Google Cloud, Best UI/UX. One winner each. A team may enter **only one** MLH track. A team can win one MLH track AND a GDG place.
- Prizes: MLH track winners get an Arduino, MLH shirt, MLH fanny pack (per member). GDG: 1st portable monitor, 2nd wallet-type prize, 3rd $25 Amazon gift card (per member).
- Google Cloud: $25 credit per team, limited (~20–30 teams), released on Discord at 12:00 first-come-first-served. (We are not entering this track.)

## What judges said per track (workshop)
- **Gemma 4**: open model from Google DeepMind. Use it through the Gemini API (key from Google AI Studio). Must be core functionality (backend logic, chatbot, image/video/voice processing), not a side add-on. **Name it in the README — if it's not there, they assume you didn't use it.** Show it in the demo video and explain how you used it.
- **UI/UX**: good UI ≠ good UX. Beautiful but hard to use loses. Must feel effortless and accessible from the first click. Explain in the demo why your UI is superior (user story + functionality). Polish it so it doesn't look AI-generated ("round corners" look); not a 2005 website either.
- **Open-Source AI**: any open model, including Gemma. Licenses must be in place in the repo. Example idea they gave: AI scam detector for leasing. Spend less time choosing a model; they care how you use it and what you build. README is very important — easier to understand = easier to score.
- **Google Cloud**: good architecture and security; show an architecture diagram (online diagram or even a drawing). Example: Firebase Auth, Cloud Storage, Gemini. Be ready to explain load balancing, deployment, autoscaling.

## GDG judging criteria (overall prize)
1. Problem validation & social impact — what problem, why it matters, impact.
2. Technical innovation & AI architecture — AI is a big chunk; must be genuinely integrated.
3. Responsible AI, ethics & accessibility — AI bounded to its intended job.
4. Feasibility, scalability & sustainability — not expected to be production-ready; idea + implementation + whether it's actually feasible.
(Point breakdown is in `partner-brief.md` / `KICKOFF_PROMPT.md` section 0.)
